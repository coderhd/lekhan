AI is turning into the line item every notes app learns to love. We went the other way.

Bring a key from OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or Sarvam — or point Lekhan at a custom endpoint. Cloud calls are relayed through our API to the provider you chose, using your key. We don't take a cut of inference, and on your own key AI never touches your plan credits.

If you'd rather keep inference off someone else's servers, run a local model with Ollama. Those requests go from your browser to your machine. They don't pass through us.

No key and no local model? The built-in Sarvam actions — chat, translate, text-to-speech, transliteration — run on a small plan-credit allowance. That is the one place a credit count exists, and I'd rather say so plainly than bury it.

The stance in one line: AI costs what your provider charges, or nothing but your hardware.

Most importers say the move worked. Then you open a page and your wikilinks are plain text.

Lekhan brings in Markdown pages and their folders, resolves links where it finds a matching page, and reports skipped files and unresolved links. Start with a small folder and compare the report to what you expected.

Keep your source vault around while you check the import. You can export your Lekhan pages back to Markdown.

# Early Access Marketing Playbook

Owner-facing strategy doc. The n8n `content-studio` workflow operationalizes this.

## Positioning line (use everywhere)

> Your second brain, your files, your AI. Local-first like Obsidian,
> collaborative like Notion — and AI runs on your own keys.

## Audience waves

1. **Founding cohort (now)**: Obsidian users curious about collaboration +
   AI. Invite-only, capped at 500, numbered spots.
2. **Notion refugees**: opens when #78 import lands (H1).
3. **Teams**: H2, after databases.

## Content pillars (rotate 1:1:1)

| Pillar | Example | Platform fit |
|---|---|---|
| Build-in-public | "This week: vault imports now preserve callouts end-to-end" + GIF | X, LinkedIn |
| Manifesto | "Your second brain shouldn't live in someone else's database." | X, LinkedIn |
| Education/PKM | "Why linked notes beat folders — and how to start in 10 minutes" | LinkedIn, blog→IG carousel |

## Rules that keep us out of trouble

1. Never post identical text across platforms — adapt voice per platform.
2. Reddit: replies only, per-thread, genuinely helpful; no links unless directly
   asked; disclosure when it's us. (Account ban history makes this mandatory.)
3. No fake scarcity (no countdown timers). Real caps only.
4. Screenshots/videos > words wherever visual platforms are involved. Budget a
   monthly recording session (OBS, 30 min).
5. Disclose AI assistance where platform norms expect it; never let the model
   invent metrics, quotes, or dates.

## Weekly operating rhythm (~90 min/week)

1. Monday: review generated draft queue (from content-studio), edit voices,
   approve.
2. Schedule approved posts in native schedulers (X Premium, LinkedIn, Meta
   Business Suite).
3. Friday: 15 min replying to every comment under our posts.
4. Monthly: record fresh demo footage; refresh screenshots.

## Metrics that matter (GA4 + platform)

- Waitlist conversion rate (visits → signups) — target >25% with clean pitch
- Referral share of signups (k-factor proxy via "referred by" field)
- Post→profile→signup attribution (UTM on bio links per platform)
- Unsubscribes/week on Brevo (canary for over-messaging)

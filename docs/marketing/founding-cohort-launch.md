## /early page copy (copywriting skill output)

**Headline:** Your notes. Your files. Your AI.

Alternatives:
- A: "Obsidian users shouldn't have to choose between their files and collaboration." (pain-first, names audience)
- B: "The second brain that stays on your disk." (bold, visual)

**Subheadline:** Lekhan imports your Obsidian vault as-is — wikilinks,
callouts, tags — then adds real-time collaboration and AI that runs on your
own keys. Free while the founding cohort lasts.

**Primary CTA button:** `Claim my founding spot`
(skill rule: name what they get; "Sign up" is weak)

Section flow, one idea each:
1. Honest scarcity: "500 spots. That's the whole cohort." (real cap, no fake counter)
2. Problem: "Collaboration tools hold your notes hostage. Note apps leave you alone."
3. Benefits, 3 bullets: files stay markdown on disk · import keeps wikilinks/callouts/tags · AI uses your key or your local model
4. How it works: Import vault → Edit together → Export anytime
5. Objection FAQ: data survival if we die · encryption status · pricing at GA
6. Final CTA repeats headline promise

Meta title: "Lekhan — Founding cohort". Meta description: "Import your
Obsidian vault as-is. Collaborate in real time. Run AI on your own keys.
500 founding spots."

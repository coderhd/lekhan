# External analysis: ChatGPT conversation on Lekhan strategy (2026-08-29)

Not a decision made here — a summary of strategic input received in a separate ChatGPT conversation (transcript held outside this repo; ask if you want it archived here too).

**Core framing offered:** Lekhan's wedge isn't "AI + Obsidian + Notion," it's "collaboration without surrendering ownership of your knowledge" — positioned against Obsidian+Sync as the real incumbent to beat, not Notion, with Anytype flagged as the most direct competitor already occupying "local-first + collaborative + private."

**Main recommendations given:**
- Protect the vault-on-disk/Tauri architecture as the single most important milestone — today's actual architecture (browser → Supabase → encrypted snapshots) is a step behind the "files on disk, collaboration layered over them" destination that is the real differentiator.
- Don't lead with AI as the wedge — treat it as an accelerator; every competitor (Notion, AFFiNE, Anytype) already has some AI story.
- Collapse the roadmap to four phases (Collaborative Markdown → Collaborative Knowledge → Knowledge AI → Team Knowledge OS) and push Sheets/Slides/Mail/Chat far out or drop them, rather than attempting to rebuild a full office suite against Notion's resources.
- Price the workspace, not AI usage — BYOK covers the AI cost directly; Lekhan charges for "the operating environment," not tokens/credits.
- The $50K ARR / 500-workspace milestone was assessed as a reasonable, achievable first target, with Obsidian's ~$25M ARR on ~9 people cited as evidence that a lean, non-team-first business can be a very good outcome without chasing Notion's scale.

**How this relates to decisions made in this log:** the roadmap-collapse and "don't lead with AI" recommendations line up with [[02-positioning-individual-pkm-first]] and [[06-thin-v1-before-deep-rag]], independently arrived at in this conversation — worth noting as convergent evidence, not because the ChatGPT conversation was the source of either decision.

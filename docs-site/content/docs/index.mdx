local-first, and AI runs on your own keys or your own machine.

## Start here

- **New to Lekhan?** Follow the [quickstart](/docs/getting-started) — it takes a few minutes.
- **Coming from Obsidian?** [Import your vault](/docs/getting-started/obsidian-import) and keep your links,
  tags, and callouts.
- **Want AI without a bill?** The [free-key on-ramp](/docs/ai/free-key-on-ramp) walks you through a
  provider's free tier. Free models may change or be slower.
- **Privacy first?** Run a model on your own machine with [BYOL](/docs/ai/byol).

## What Lekhan is not

Lekhan does not bundle AI credits and never proxies your AI traffic. Every plan runs AI on your own
provider key or your local endpoint — your quota, your data, your call.

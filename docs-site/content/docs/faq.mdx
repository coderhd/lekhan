## Do I need an account?

You can write, search, import, and export in the local browser workspace without an account. Sign in to create or share cloud pages.

## Does signing in sync my local notes?

Your local guest and account workspaces have separate browser storage. Export the guest notes as Markdown and import them into the account or cloud workspace where you want them.

## Can I keep a copy outside Lekhan?

Download a page as Markdown. The local workspace also exports a ZIP of Markdown pages arranged in folders.

## Does the assistant receive my current page?

The assistant sends your prompt to the connection you chose. It includes the page only when you enable **Include this page with my request**.

See [Local and cloud workspaces](/docs/concepts/workspaces), [Markdown import and export](/docs/getting-started/markdown-import-export), and [AI connections](/docs/ai).

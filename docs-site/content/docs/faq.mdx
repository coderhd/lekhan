## Is my data really local-first?

Yes. Lekhan writes your pages to a local replica and syncs through the hub. You can open, edit, and
close pages offline; changes merge when you reconnect. Markdown is the portable form of your content,
so you are never locked in.

## Does AI cost extra?

No. There are no bundled AI credits on any plan. Every tier runs AI on your own provider key or your
own machine, so AI usage is billed by the provider you choose — or free, if you use a free tier or a
local model. Lekhan never proxies inference.

## What is the difference between BYOK and BYOL?

- **BYOK**: cloud provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, Sarvam, custom) with your API key.
- **BYOL**: a model running on your own machine via Ollama, LM Studio, or llama.cpp. Prompts never
  leave your device.

See [AI on your terms](/docs/ai).

## What happens to my notes if I stop paying?

Your content stays yours and remains exportable in markdown. Paid plans change what is *unlocked*
(sync, collaborators, history, seats) — they never hold your pages hostage.

## Can I import from Obsidian and Notion?

- **Obsidian**: import a vault ZIP or folder. Links, tags, callouts, and frontmatter are preserved,
  and an import report tells you what degraded. See [Obsidian import](/docs/getting-started/obsidian-import).
- **Notion**: export to markdown and import the files; rich clipboard HTML from Notion is also
  understood when you paste.

## How does version history work?

Pages keep point-in-time versions according to your plan: 7 days on Free, 90 days on Plus, one year
on Pro, and a year on Team. Retention is enforced per workspace.

## What are the plans and prices?

Prices are per workspace. Founding-cohort prices are locked for as long as you stay subscribed;
standard prices apply to later joiners. Annual billing charges for ten months — two months free.

| Plan | Monthly (USD) | Monthly (INR) | Unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | ₹0 | Unlimited local pages, PKM core (links, backlinks, graph, tags, search), markdown, AI on your own key, 2 editors per page, 7-day history |
| Plus | $6 standard / $4 founding | ₹499 standard / ₹249 founding | Cloud sync, 10 collaborators, 90-day history, templates |
| Pro | $12 standard / $8 founding | ₹999 standard / ₹499 founding | 25 collaborators, 1-year history, priority sync and processing, public sites |
| Team | $10 standard / $8 founding per seat | USD only at launch | Per-seat billing, admin, shared templates, guest access, 2–150 seats |

Team seat pricing steps down at higher volumes ($7/seat for 11–25, $6/seat for 26+). Team is
USD-only at launch; INR individuals use Plus or Pro.

## Do referrals really give free months?

Yes. When someone you invite activates, one month is banked on your workspace; when they upgrade to a
paid plan, a second month is banked. Banked months apply at renewal, stack with annual savings, and
are not cash or transferable. The bank is capped per workspace.

## Can I transfer a workspace to someone else?

The owner can transfer ownership to another member. The subscription follows the workspace, and the
previous owner's auto-billing is stopped before the transfer completes.

## Is my content encrypted?

Content is encrypted at rest on hub storage with server-held keys. An opt-in end-to-end mode with
client-held keys is a separate, stronger level and trades away some hub-side indexing features. AI
keys are always encrypted locally (AES-256-GCM), separate from page storage.

## Does Lekhan work offline?

Yes for pages: editing, navigation, and local search work offline. Cloud sync, collaboration, and
cloud AI require a connection; local AI (BYOL) works offline once the model is running.

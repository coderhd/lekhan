---
title: Share and edit a cloud page
description: Invite viewers or editors, manage public access, and restore a checkpoint.
---

## Invite a collaborator

1. Sign in and open **Cloud workspace**. Select a page you own.
2. Open **Share**, enter the recipient’s email, and choose **Can view** or **Can edit**.
3. Create the invitation and send its copied link to the recipient. They sign in with the invited email and accept.
4. To change access later, return to **Share** and update their role or remove them.

Each page can have two collaborators on Free, ten on Plus, or twenty-five on Pro. On Team, the workspace’s purchased seats set the limit. Each account and pending invitation uses one place. Cancel a pending invitation or remove a collaborator to make room. A downgrade keeps existing access and pauses new invitations when the page is at its new limit.

## Reopen or search cloud notes

Open a cloud page online in a browser that supports service workers to save a copy on that device. If the hosted app becomes unreachable, reopen the original page URL or use **On this device** from the cloud workspace to browse saved pages. You can write in pages you could edit when they were last checked, reload the browser, and download Markdown. Saved pages retain their viewing or editing access until Lekhan can check it again.

Stay signed in to the same account on that browser to open its copies. Lekhan rechecks access when the connection returns and syncs your edits when you still have editing access. Open the hosted page to use sharing, history, and attached files.

Use **Search pages** to find cloud notes by title, text, tag, or link. Matches from copies on this device can appear while the rest of the cloud search loads. Those copies belong to your signed-in account, and the hosted workspace checks whether you still have access.

## Publish a read-only link

Open **Share → Link access** and choose **Make link public**. The public reader shows the saved page. Use **Turn off public link** to revoke access for visitors. Existing invited access is managed under **People with access**.

## Add an image

Choose **Add image** in the editor. Free cloud pages resize and convert images to WebP, up to 1,600 pixels on the longest side and 500 KB per image. Paid plans preserve supported image files up to 20 MB. If an image pauses sync, your draft stays available on this device. Remove the image and choose **Retry sync** to share your edits.

## Attach a file

Open **Attachments** on a cloud page and choose **Attach a file**. Free pages accept up to 2 MB per file. Paid pages accept up to 50 MB per file. Invited viewers can download files. Page owners can remove a file for everyone with access to the page.

Open **Workspace billing** to see saved inline images and file attachments. The 30 MB guide on Free is advisory. Paid workspaces have a combined 10 GB allowance.

## Save a checkpoint

When a page is connected, open **Version history** and choose **Save checkpoint**. Open a checkpoint to preview its text and images. **Restore and keep a backup** saves the current content before restoring the older version.

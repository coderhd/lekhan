## Sync and offline

**I edited offline — will my changes come back?**
Yes. Edits are applied to your local replica first and merge when the connection returns. Keep the tab
open until the sync indicator settles, then reopen the page to confirm.

**A page looks stale on another device.**
Check the sync indicator first. If it stays disconnected, reload the app; pending local changes are
replayed on reconnect. If two devices edited the same block offline, Lekhan merges the underlying
CRDT state — review the result rather than expecting one side to win.

**I see a version conflict banner.**
That is the merge being surfaced, not data loss. Open the page, review the merged content, and use
version history if you need to compare with an earlier snapshot.

## AI

**The connection test fails.**
- Re-check the key: copy it again from the provider dashboard and paste it without trailing spaces.
- Confirm the model is available to that key (free models are often the first to be withdrawn).
- For BYOK, confirm your network allows requests to the provider's API domain.
- For BYOL, confirm the local server is running and the base URL ends in `/v1`.

**AI works on one device but not another.**
Keys are encrypted locally per browser. Paste the key again on the new device — Lekhan cannot sync or
recover it.

**Free models are slow or unavailable.**
Free tiers are rate-limited and change without notice. Try another free preset or add your own key.
Free models may change or be slower; this is not a Lekhan outage.

**I do not want AI at all.**
Do not connect a provider. Every page, import, and export works without AI.

## Imports

**"No markdown files found."**
Select the folder that directly contains your notes, not its parent and not an export wrapper. For a
ZIP, make sure the notes are inside it rather than only attachments.

**Some links did not resolve.**
Links resolve by page title. If the target note was not in the import, the link stays unresolved
until you create that page. The import report lists link resolution counts.

**An image or embed is missing.**
Attachments that could not be read are reported as degraded and may appear as links instead. Large
attachments may exceed the import limits.

**Can I import the same vault twice?**
You can, but you will get duplicate pages. Import into a fresh workspace if you want a clean copy.

## Search and graph

**A new page is not in search or the graph yet.**
The index updates as you type. Wait for sync to settle, then search again. Unresolved links still
appear as edges even when the target page does not exist.

## Billing

**I upgraded but do not see the new limits.**
Refresh after checkout completes and confirm the plan on the workspace billing page. Plan changes
attach to the workspace, so make sure you are looking at the right one.

**How do I cancel?**
Open the workspace billing page and cancel. You keep access through the end of the paid period, and
your pages remain exportable.

**I was charged in a different currency than expected.**
USD is handled by Stripe and INR by Razorpay based on your billing country. Team is USD-only at
launch.

## Still stuck?

Check the [FAQ](/docs/faq), then contact support with your workspace name, the page involved, and the
approximate time of the problem.

title: Quickstart

This guide takes you from a new account to a workspace with your first page, a link, and AI
connected. You do not need a credit card, and you do not need to bring an AI key to start writing.

## 1. Create your workspace

A workspace is the container for your knowledge graph. It holds your pages and is the unit of ownership
and billing; sharing happens per page today.

1. Sign up and name your workspace.
2. The first page opens automatically. If you already have notes, skip ahead to
   [import](/docs/getting-started/obsidian-import).

<Callout title="Local-first by default">
Your pages are written to a local replica first. The hub syncs changes between your devices when a
connection is available, and you can keep editing offline.
</Callout>

## 2. Write your first page

Every page is a node in the graph. Type `/` to open the block menu, or just start writing.

- **Bold**, *italic*, lists, tables, code blocks, and callouts all round-trip to markdown.
- Press `[[` to link another page by title. If the page does not exist yet, the link is kept as
  unresolved until you create it.
- Add `#tags` inline to make a page easy to filter later.

## 3. Connect the graph

- [Page links and backlinks](/docs/concepts/links-and-backlinks) explain how `[[...]]`
  links create the graph.
- [Tags](/docs/concepts/tags) group pages across folders.
- [Graph](/docs/concepts/graph) is the visual view over those connections.

## 4. Connect AI (optional)

AI is never required to use Lekhan. When you want it:

- **No key yet?** Use the [free-key provider guide](/docs/ai/free-key-on-ramp) to connect a provider's
  free tier.
- **Already have a key?** Follow [BYOK](/docs/ai/byok) for Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, DeepSeek, Qwen,
  Z.AI, Groq, OpenRouter, Sarvam, or a custom endpoint.
- **Want everything local?** Follow [BYOL](/docs/ai/byol) for Ollama or LM Studio.

<Callout type="info" title="No bundled credits, ever">
Every plan runs AI on your own provider key or your local endpoint. Lekhan never meters AI usage and
never stores prompts as AI logs. Free models may change or be slower — their limits are the provider's.
</Callout>

## 5. Bring your existing notes

- Paste markdown straight into a page, or follow
  [Markdown import and export](/docs/getting-started/markdown-import-export).
- Move an Obsidian vault with [Obsidian import](/docs/getting-started/obsidian-import).

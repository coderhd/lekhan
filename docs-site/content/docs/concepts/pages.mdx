---
title: Pages
description: Create and organize writing as pages with titles, links, and subpages.
---

A page is the unit you write, link, search, and export. A page can contain Markdown, rich text blocks, or links to other pages.

In the local workspace, choose **New page** to create a top-level page. Open **Page actions → New subpage** to add a page under the current one, or **Move page** to choose a different parent.

In the cloud workspace, create a page under **My pages**. Owners can add a subpage or change its title under **Page settings**. Membership and public visibility are set from the page’s **Share** screen.

See [Page links and backlinks](/docs/concepts/links-and-backlinks) and [Collaboration](/docs/guides/collaboration).

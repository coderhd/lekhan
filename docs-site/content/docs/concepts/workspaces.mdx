A **workspace** is the root container of your knowledge graph. It is the unit of ownership,
collaboration, and billing.

## One workspace, one graph

Everything inside a workspace shares one graph: pages, links, tags, search, and the graph view.
Members of a workspace can navigate the same pages according to their role.

## Members and roles

Invite people by email from the workspace members area. Roles control what someone can do:

| Role | Can read | Can edit pages | Manage workspace and billing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Owner | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Editor | Yes | Yes | No |
| Viewer | Yes | No | No |

The owner holds the subscription. Ownership can be transferred to another member when you leave a
team — the subscription follows the workspace, not the person.

## Billing is per workspace

- **Free** workspaces are fully usable offline and locally.
- **Plus** and **Pro** attach to the workspace and unlock cloud sync, more collaborators, longer
  history, and (on Pro) public sites.
- **Team** adds per-seat billing, admin, shared templates, and guest access.

See [Plans and billing](/docs/faq) for the full tier matrix.

## Workspaces and vaults

A workspace is Lekhan's owning container. A **vault** is an external Obsidian folder you import —
Lekhan copies it into a workspace and never writes back to the original folder. See
[Obsidian import](/docs/getting-started/obsidian-import).

---
title: Local and cloud workspaces
description: Understand where each copy is stored and when it is shared.
---

| Where you write | Where it is saved | How you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guest local workspace | IndexedDB in the current browser and site origin | Open the workspace without signing in. |
| Account local workspace | A separate IndexedDB replica for the signed-in account | Sign in, then open the workspace. Paid workspaces sync saved notes automatically. |
| Cloud workspace | Cloud pages and an account-scoped browser copy after opening the page | Sign in, open **Cloud workspace**, then create or import a page. |

Your guest notes stay in the guest workspace when you sign in. On Free, open **Page actions → Sync this page** in your signed-in local workspace to copy a selected note. On a paid plan, the local workspace starts syncing your saved notes when you’re online. A note you’re actively editing waits until you leave the editor. An interrupted transfer resumes when you reopen the workspace or reconnect.

Sync includes the files attached to the note. Free workspaces accept files up to 2 MB each, and paid workspaces accept files up to 50 MB each. A note with an unsupported file stays in your notebook. After an interrupted file transfer, the notebook may ask you to wait for cloud storage before trying again.

**Open synced page** takes you to the cloud editor after the note and its files are saved. The original local Markdown snapshot remains available under **Page actions → Export local snapshot**. You can download individual files in page context, or export the notebook ZIP to keep copies of pages and files together. You can also use the cloud workspace’s import action to bring across a folder or vault.

## Reopen the local notebook during an outage

After opening the workspace online, you can revisit its URL during an outage in the same browser. The **Offline notebook** opens your guest notebook or the notebook for the account last verified in that browser. You can keep writing, search the notebook, and export a copy. If you’ve switched accounts, sign in online before opening that account’s notebook.

When service returns, choose **Open notebook online** in the sidebar. It opens the same local draft and resumes any eligible cloud sync.

If you sync a subpage first, it appears at the top level until you sync its parent. Once both are synced, the cloud workspace places the subpage under its parent.

You can work on a previously opened cloud page offline when its browser copy and writing access are available. The status bar shows when the connection is live again. Keep an export before clearing site data.

On Free, use **Pause cloud sync** on a personal cloud page to keep new writing on this device. Other devices keep the last synced version they already opened. Choose **Resume cloud sync** to share your local edits again. Shared and published pages stay connected, as do pages in paid workspaces.

A **workspace** is the root container of your knowledge graph. It is the unit of ownership and billing,
and it is where your pages live.

## One workspace, one graph

Everything inside a workspace shares one graph: pages, links, tags, search, and the graph view.

## Sharing is per page

Collaboration today is scoped to individual pages, not to the workspace. Open a page and use **Share**
to invite someone by email; the invite applies to that page only.

| Role | Can read the page | Can edit the page | Can manage access |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Owner | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Editor | Yes | Yes | No |
| Viewer | Yes | No | No |

The page owner controls access. **Settings → Collaborators & Access** lists the pages you own so you can
review and revoke access without opening each one.

<Callout type="info" title="Workspace-level roles are planned">
Workspace-wide members, roles, and ownership transfer ship with workspace billing. Until then every
invitation is page-scoped, and the only way to hand over a workspace is to contact support.
</Callout>

## Billing is per workspace

- **Free** workspaces are fully usable offline and locally.
- **Plus** and **Pro** attach to the workspace and unlock cloud sync, more collaborators, longer
  history, and (on Pro) public sites.
- **Team** adds per-seat billing, admin, shared templates, and guest access.

See [Plans and billing](/docs/faq) for the full tier matrix, and **Settings → Billing** in the app to
manage the plan.

## Workspaces and vaults

A workspace is Lekhan's owning container. A **vault** is an external Obsidian folder you import —
Lekhan copies it into a workspace and never writes back to the original folder. See
[Obsidian import](/docs/getting-started/obsidian-import).

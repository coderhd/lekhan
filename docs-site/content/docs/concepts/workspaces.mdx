---
title: Local and cloud workspaces
description: Understand where each copy is stored and when it is shared.
---

| Where you write | Where it is saved | How you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guest local workspace | IndexedDB in the current browser and site origin | Open the workspace without signing in. |
| Account local workspace | A separate IndexedDB replica for the signed-in account | Sign in, then open the workspace. |
| Cloud workspace | Cloud pages and an account-scoped browser copy after opening the page | Sign in, open **Cloud workspace**, then create or import a page. |

Your guest notes stay in the guest workspace when you sign in. In your signed-in local workspace, open **Page actions → Sync this page** to copy a selected note to your cloud workspace. **Open synced page** takes you to its cloud editor. The original local snapshot remains available under **Page actions → Export local snapshot**. To bring across a whole folder or vault, use the cloud workspace’s import action.

If you sync a subpage first, it appears at the top level until you sync its parent. Once both are synced, the cloud workspace places the subpage under its parent.

You can work on a previously opened cloud page offline when its browser copy and writing access are available. The status bar shows when the connection is live again. Keep an export before clearing site data.

On Free, use **Pause cloud sync** on a personal cloud page to keep new writing on this device. Other devices keep the last synced version they already opened. Choose **Resume cloud sync** to share your local edits again. Shared and published pages stay connected, as do pages in paid workspaces.

See [Markdown import and export](/docs/getting-started/markdown-import-export) and [Collaboration](/docs/guides/collaboration).

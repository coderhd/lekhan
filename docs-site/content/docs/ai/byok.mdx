---
title: BYOK Setup
description: Bring your own cloud provider key and keep it encrypted on your device.
---

**BYOK** (bring your own key) connects a cloud AI provider with a key you control. Lekhan calls the
provider directly from your browser; your key and prompts never pass through Lekhan's servers.

## Supported providers

- Anthropic
- OpenAI
- Google Gemini
- Sarvam
- Any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (custom base URL)

You can configure more than one provider and switch between them per request.

## Steps

1. Create an API key in your provider's dashboard. Give it the narrowest scope that works for you.
2. Open **Settings → AI** and choose the provider.
3. Paste the key. Lekhan encrypts it with AES-256-GCM before storing it in your browser.
4. Pick a model and send a test prompt.

## What Lekhan stores

- The **encrypted** key, in your browser's local storage.
- Your provider and model selection.
- Nothing else: prompts and responses are not persisted as AI logs.

<Callout type="info" title="Encrypted locally">
The decryption material stays on your device. If you clear site data or use another browser, you
will need to paste the key again. Lekhan cannot recover a key for you.
</Callout>

## Security checklist

- Use a dedicated key for Lekhan rather than a personal master key.
- Set a spend limit at the provider if it offers one.
- Rotate the key in the provider dashboard, then replace it in **Settings → AI**.
- Remove a key in Lekhan when you stop using a provider; removal does not affect your pages.

## Custom endpoints

For a provider that speaks the OpenAI API, choose **Custom** and enter the base URL (for example an
internal gateway). The same encryption and direct-call rules apply.

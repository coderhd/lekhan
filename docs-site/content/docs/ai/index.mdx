Lekhan's AI is a **provider registry**, not a hosted assistant. You connect the providers you trust,
and every request goes directly from your browser to that provider (or to a model on your own
machine). Lekhan never proxies inference and never hosts an inference key.

## The rules, stated plainly

- **No bundled AI credits on any tier.** AI usage is never a Lekhan line item.
- **No proxying.** Your key and your prompts travel to the provider you choose, not through Lekhan.
- **No metering.** Lekhan does not count tokens or cap AI usage; the provider's quota applies.
- **Keys are encrypted locally** (AES-256-GCM) before they are stored in your browser.
- **Free models may change or be slower.** Free-tier availability is controlled by the provider.

## Choosing a path

| If you… | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| Have never made an API key | [Free-key on-ramp](/docs/ai/free-key-on-ramp) |
| Already pay for a provider | [BYOK](/docs/ai/byok) |
| Want prompts to stay on your device | [BYOL](/docs/ai/byol) |
| Are on a locked-down network | BYOK (cloud) or BYOL (local server) |

You can switch paths at any time. Connecting a local model does not remove your cloud keys, and
removing a key never touches your pages.

## Where AI appears

Once a provider is connected, the assistant is available in the editor. You can switch providers and
models per request, so a quick draft can use a fast model while a careful edit uses a stronger one.

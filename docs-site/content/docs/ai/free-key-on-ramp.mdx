---
title: Free-Key Providers
description: Connect a free-tier AI provider without a credit card.
---

There is no separate signup flow for free AI: you connect a provider in **Settings → AI**, exactly like
any other BYOK setup. What makes it an *on-ramp* is that several providers offer a usable free tier, so
you can start without a card. You issue the key at the provider and paste it into Lekhan's encrypted key
store.

<Callout type="warning" title="Free models may change or be slower">
Free tiers are controlled by the provider. Models can be renamed, rate-limited, or withdrawn, and
free requests are often slower than paid ones. Lekhan cannot guarantee availability or speed of a
free model.
</Callout>

## What you need

- An account with a provider that offers a free tier.
- A few minutes; no card should be required for the providers below.

## Providers with a free tier

These are the presets the app points at today:

| Provider | Free tier | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Gemini API | Free tier | Per-project quota |
| Groq | Free tier | Fast inference on selected models |
| DeepSeek | Free tier | Selected models |
| Z.AI | Free tier | Selected models |
| OpenRouter | Free models | One key, many models; free models are labelled |

Any other OpenAI-compatible provider works through [BYOK](/docs/ai/byok) with a custom base URL.
Presets change as providers change their free offerings.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → AI** and pick the provider row (for example Gemini or Groq).
2. Follow the provider's key page, create a key, and copy it.
3. Paste the key into Lekhan and choose a free model from that provider.
4. Send a test prompt to confirm the connection. If it fails, check
   [AI troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting).

## Good to know

- Your rate limits are **the provider's**, not Lekhan's.
- Cloud requests are relayed through Lekhan's API using your key; prompts are not stored as AI logs
  and usage is never metered.
- You can add a paid key later without redoing your setup — the key store supports multiple
  providers.
- There is no Lekhan-hosted "trial AI" and no bundled credit allowance.

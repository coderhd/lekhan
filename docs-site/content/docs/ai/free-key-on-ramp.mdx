title: Free-Key On-Ramp
description: Connect a free-tier AI provider without a credit card.
---

The **free-key on-ramp** is a guided version of BYOK for people who have never created an API key.
Lekhan helps you pick a provider with a free tier, issue a key, and paste it into the encrypted key
store — then you are running AI on your own quota.

<Callout type="warning" title="Free models may change or be slower">
Free tiers are controlled by the provider. Models can be renamed, rate-limited, or withdrawn, and
free requests are often slower than paid ones. Lekhan cannot guarantee availability or speed of a
free model.
</Callout>

## What you need

- An account with a provider that offers a free tier.
- A few minutes; no card should be required for the presets below.

## Presets

The wizard offers curated presets, including:

| Provider | Free tier | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OpenRouter | Free models | One key, many models; free models are labelled |
| Google Gemini API | Free tier | Per-project quota |
| Groq | Free tier | Fast inference on selected models |
| Mistral | Free tier | Selected models |

Presets change as providers change their free offerings. If a preset is unavailable, use
[BYOK](/docs/ai/byok) with any OpenAI-compatible provider.

## Steps

1. Open the AI connection wizard (it appears on first AI use, or from **Settings → AI**).
2. Pick a provider preset and follow the deep link to its key page.
3. Create a key, copy it, and paste it into Lekhan. The key is encrypted locally before storage.
4. Send a test prompt to confirm the connection. If it fails, check
   [AI troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting).

## Good to know

- Your rate limits are **the provider's**, not Lekhan's.
- Requests go directly from your browser to the provider. Lekhan does not see your prompts.
- You can add a paid key later without redoing your setup — the key store supports multiple
  providers.
- The free tier of Lekhan includes the on-ramp. There is no Lekhan-hosted "trial AI".

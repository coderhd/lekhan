# Lekhan — Product Marketing Context

Shared context consumed by marketing skills (launch, copywriting, social, emails…).
Keep current; single source of truth for voice and claims.

## What Lekhan is

A local-first collaborative knowledge workspace. Pages live as a graph
(backlinks, tags, search), sync offline-first via CRDT, support real-time
multiplayer editing, and every AI feature runs on the user's own keys or local
models — never on our servers.

## Positioning line

Your second brain, your files, your AI. Local-first like Obsidian,
collaborative like Notion — AI runs on your own keys.

## Primary audience (founding cohort)

Obsidian users who want real-time collaboration and working AI without cloud
lock-in or per-feature AI fees. Technical-adjacent, privacy-aware, allergic to
marketing fluff. They already own their notes in markdown — we import them
faithfully, including callouts, wikilinks, and frontmatter.

## Objections to address

- "Will my notes survive if you die?" → markdown export, open vault format,
  honest import/export reports.
- "AI here means subscription creep" → BYOK/BYOL/free-key presets; we never
  host inference or meter credits.
- "Another Notion clone?" → local-first is architectural, not a checkbox;
  your files stay on your disk.
- "Is my data encrypted?" → encrypted at rest by default (E2E tier planned).
